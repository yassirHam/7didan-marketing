5. Answerability: Prioritize passages that provide reliable evidence for constructing a precise, factual, and well-grounded answer.

- A passage addressing only one part of a multi-part question may still be relevant, but should not be treated as covering the entire information need.
- Distinguish between directly useful evidence, indirectly related information, and irrelevant content.
- Do not reward a passage merely because it contains overlapping keywords or mentions the same product.
